A working prototype, not a concept

Every answer is signed and hash-chained at the seat. The system survives relay, cell and link failures, and any tampering afterwards is locatable and recoverable.

The five demo acts, in exam order. Each drives real seat, relay, cell and control processes (not mocks) and writes an evidence file to docs/evidence/.

Repository: https://github.com/anirudh12032008/saakshi · Tested on macOS and Windows. The design never auto-penalises a candidate: flags go to a human reviewer.

Key limit, stated up front: a seat signature proves a record was not altered after the device produced it, not that the candidate chose it. Analytics figures use synthetic data and performance figures are loopback on one host.
