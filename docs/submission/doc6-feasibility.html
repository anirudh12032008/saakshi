6 · Feasibility

Checklist of what is done

  • Seat app signs and saves every answer, even offline
  • Relay in the centre cannot read answers
  • Three cells verify and countersign; a wiped cell rebuilds from relays
  • Spare relay takes over during an exam
  • Paper released only when 2 of 3 custodians agree; offline unlock code works
  • Candidate moves to another seat and keeps the time they had
  • Audit page shows exactly which record was edited
  • Analytics flags likely cheating; no honest candidate flagged (synthetic data)
  • 476 workspace tests and 76 analytics tests pass
Why it can be implemented. It runs on today's centre PCs, uses standard cryptography (SHA-256, ECDSA P-256), and is open source under Apache-2.0 with a frozen protocol and fixed test vectors.
