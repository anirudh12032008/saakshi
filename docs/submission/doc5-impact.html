5 · IMPACT · WHO GAINS

Who gains

An online exam that keeps working when things break, and can prove what happened.

CANDIDATES
  • Answers are kept: 0 of 16,000 lost in chaos drills
  • A cut network does not end the exam
  • Only those truly hurt are re-tested
  • Notices in Hindi, English and Tamil
NTA AND GOVERNMENT
  • Any edit to a record is found: 1,000 of 1,000 tests
  • Clear choice: compensate, re-test or re-conduct
  • Less doubt, fewer court cases
  • Fewer full re-exams
EXAM CENTRES
  • Bad seats are caught before the exam
  • Recovery takes about 1 to 2 seconds
  • A centre with a cut link can still start, with a phoned code
SOCIETY
  • Honest candidates are not punished
  • Cheating is found, not guessed
  • Hindi and Tamil access, not English only

5 · IMPACT · THE DECISION

The decision, in numbers

One Act 5 run on a full size 20,000 candidate test cohort. After a cheating leak at 3 centres and an outage, Saakshi worked out who needs what. The data is synthetic.

18,994candidates spared a re-test
₹2,84,91,000rupees avoided
225candidates re-tested
3centres re-conducted
322flags raised for review
55flags backed by history
Rupees avoided is 18,994 candidates times ₹1,500. The ₹1,500 is an example policy value, not an NTA figure. The report prints this assumption.

5 · IMPACT · FAIRNESS

Fair to honest candidates

  • Past history alone never flags anyone. It only backs up a flag.
  • In the 2,000 candidate run, no honest candidate was flagged.
  • A person signs off every decision.

5 · IMPACT · LANGUAGES

Notices in three languages

After a decision, each affected candidate gets a notice they can read. The notice is approved first, then shown in Hindi, English and Tamil.

हिन्दी
Hindi notice and banner
ENGLISH
English notice and banner
தமிழ்
Tamil notice, banner and status page
The Hindi and Tamil drafts are machine translated. Native speakers must review them before real use. Tamil question text is still English until a Tamil question bank exists.

5 · IMPACT · LIMITS

What we do not claim

  • All numbers come from synthetic data and one Mac. They show the method works, not a real national result.
  • A signature proves a record was not changed after the seat made it. It does not prove what the candidate meant.
  • Tested on macOS and Windows.
