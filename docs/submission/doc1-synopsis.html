Saakshi (साक्षी, "witness") in one page

The problem

Online exams can fail, and when they do, nobody can prove what really happened.

Our solution

Sign every answer at the seat, keep going through failures, and make any tampering easy to find and fix.

1 · SOLUTION SYNOPSIS

Three promises

We cannot promise zero failures. We make every failure:

Recoverable

Lose a cell, a relay or a link and nothing is lost.

Contained

One broken part stays one broken part. Other centres carry on.

Provable

Any edit after the fact is located, and the true answer is shown.

Tested on macOS and Windows. Code and evidence: https://github.com/anirudh12032008/saakshi

1 · SOLUTION SYNOPSIS

The five demo acts

Each act runs real software, not mocks, and leaves an evidence file.

ActWhat we showResult
1 Before
Gate check		A seat running AnyDesk and an overlay tool is blocked by name. Close them and it turns green. A face flag goes to a human reviewer.2 tools named and blocked. 1 flag cleared by a person.
2 Start
Key release		2 of 3 key holders release the paper keys. 6 of 7 centres unlock. Centre 42 has a cut link, so it stays locked until a phoned code unlocks it. A forged key is rejected.308 candidates, 7 centres, 6,586 entries saved.
3 During
Chaos		We cut a centre's link, wipe a data centre cell, and force-quit a seat. Candidates keep answering. The wiped cell rebuilds. The seat resumes on another PC.Answers lost: 0. Recovery 1.3 to 1.9 s.
4 After
Tamper audit		A rogue insider edits an answer in the database. The audit finds it. The verify page shows "record says C, the seat committed B" and restores B.1,000 of 1,000 edits located.
5 Decide
Analytics		Analytics on the cells' own data flag likely cheating and choose: pay compensation, re-test, or re-conduct a centre. A human always signs off. No candidate is penalised automatically.53 flags, no honest candidate flagged (synthetic data).
Tested on macOS and Windows. Code and evidence: https://github.com/anirudh12032008/saakshi
